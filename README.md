## Hello - I am Alys 👋

I’m a quantitative researcher working where data science meets ecology and public health. I build models from big, spatial and sometimes messy data, and I like making the results easy for people to use.

🔭 What I work on

- Mapping the world’s ecosystems from satellite data for the [Global Ecosystems Atlas](https://github.com/Global-Ecosystems-Atlas) ([Global Ecology Lab](https://www.globalecologylab.org/) at [James Cook University](https://www.jcu.edu.au/))
- Real-time situational assessment and forecasting of epidemics for health authorities in Australia and New Zealand ([ACEFA](https://acefa-hubs.github.io/) at [The University of Melbourne](https://www.unimelb.edu.au/).
)
- Bringing Indigenous knowledge and global data together to classify and map ecosystems (my PhD, Deakin University)
- Testing ecosystem mapping models [see preprint](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/view/8500/) and developing ecosystem information with Indigenous and local knowledge holders ([see publication](https://conbio.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/cobi.70099))
- Identifying management priorities for threatened species and planning decisions, like [drought refuges](https://zslpublications.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/acv.12763), [restoration priorities](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2352485523000464), [spatial decision support tools](https://livingshorelines.shinyapps.io/VicMap/)

**Tools I use most**

R (tidyverse, sf, terra, Shiny, Quarto), models (GLMs/GLMMs/GAMs, occupancy models, random forests), and Google Earth Engine.
