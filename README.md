## Hello - I am Alys 👋

I am a quantitative ecologist passionate about conserving biodiversity.
 
My research focuses on biogeography, ecology, and remote sensing, often using large spatial datasets. I am a Post-doctoral Research Fellow with the [Global Ecology Lab](https://www.globalecologylab.org/) at [James Cook University](https://www.jcu.edu.au/) working on the [Global Ecosystems Atlas](https://github.com/Global-Ecosystems-Atlas) and a research assistant with [ACEFA](https://acefa-hubs.github.io/) at [The University of Melbourne](https://www.unimelb.edu.au/).

🔭 My work spans a range of biodiversity issues and quantitative approaches, including:

- Identifying refuges for threatened species ([see publication](https://zslpublications.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/acv.12763))
- Modelling species distribution to inform restoration ([see publication](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2352485523000464) and [shiny app](https://livingshorelines.shinyapps.io/VicMap/))
- Investigating trends in animal populations in central Australia ([see related publication](https://www.publish.csiro.au/wr/WR24070))
- Advancing the development of ecosystem information ([see publication](https://conbio.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/cobi.70099))
- Testing quantitative approaches to ecosystem mapping ([see preprint](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/view/8500/))
